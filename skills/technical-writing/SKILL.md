---
name: technical-writing
description: Use when writing reports, docs, summaries, PR or commit text, or notebook prose. Plain, specific writing without AI patterns.
---

- Lead with the answer, then the evidence. One idea per paragraph.
- Numbers over adjectives: "3 files, 2 min", not "a few files, quick".
- Name things the way the SPEC glossary does. Define a term once.
- State limits and what was not checked.
- Cut: filler openers, hedging stacks, "it is important to note", rhetorical questions, lists of three for rhythm, summaries that repeat the body.
- Prefer a table when 2 or more items have 2 or more attributes. Prefer short sentences.
- Read it as the reader who has 30 seconds. If the first line is not the answer, rewrite it.
